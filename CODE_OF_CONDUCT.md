# FinanceSharp Code of Conduct

FinanceSharp welcomes participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. This code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and make space for different experiences and viewpoints.
- Give constructive, specific feedback about the work. Accept corrections and explain disagreements using evidence.
- Respect people's time, boundaries, and privacy.
- Help keep technical discussions useful and accessible to newcomers.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable. Repeated disruption or retaliation against someone who raises a concern is also unacceptable.

## Raising a concern

Contact the [repository maintainer](https://github.com/Nucs) using a contact method they make publicly available. If no suitable private method is listed, open an issue requesting a private contact channel without including personal details or the substance of the report. GitHub's own reporting tools can also be used to report abusive content or behavior on the platform.

Do not post sensitive reports or another person's private information in a public issue. Include relevant links and context when a private channel has been established.

## Moderation

Maintainers may remove or edit inappropriate content, ask participants to change their behavior, restrict participation, or block participants, depending on the circumstances. Moderation should be proportionate and consistent, with attention to the privacy of the people involved. Participants may ask a maintainer to review a moderation decision through the same contact process.
